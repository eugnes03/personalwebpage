About Me

I am Eugen, a masters student from Norway attending Boston University.

I study Mathematics, with aspirations of eventually becoming a PhD candidate. This website is my attempt to keep up with mathematical research, my classes, my extracurriculars, and to remind myself that iterative learning is the best way for me to learn.

My current interests within Mathematics is in algebra and probability, where I am currently learning about Representation Theory, Probability Theory and Neural Networks. My goal over the next few months is to reconcile these areas of maths into a research project.

In addition to studies, I am an avid tennis and chess player. I feel it is just as important to stay active as it is to learn (often times they go hand in hand). Feel free to contact me about anything! Questions, concerns, comments!

The notebooks on this website are written in Clojure and rendered using Clay and Quarto, inspired by the Clojure Civitas community.
